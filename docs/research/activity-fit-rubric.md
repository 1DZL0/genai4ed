# Does the lesson fit its activity? A rubric

This rubric is for the researchers who judge whether a lesson the agent drafted fits the activity
it carries: its heading and its kind. It judges the lesson, not the agent's sentence about it.
Use the lesson card (or the lesson's brief) and its row in `lesson_activity.csv`. Judge before you
look at how the teacher responded to the guess.

## Five points, each scored 0, 1 or 2

1. **The students do the kind.** The steps ask the students to do what the kind says: an essay
   lesson has them write an essay; a multiple-choice lesson gives options to choose from; an
   inquiry lesson has them form a hypothesis, plan or read results.
   2: every step; 1: most steps; 0: few or none.
2. **The finish lines judge what the kind needs.** A kind judged against criteria has finish lines
   that are criteria for the work. A right-answers kind has finish lines that name the answer.
   2: all steps; 1: some; 0: none, or the wrong sort throughout.
3. **The lesson has the heading's shape.** It follows the line "Such a lesson: ..." for its
   heading in the coding guide's list (for writing: a brief, a plan, a draft and a revision).
   2: clearly; 1: in part; 0: not at all.
4. **The teacher's own words are kept.** Every step, question, finish line and rule the teacher
   wrote is there unchanged. A teacher who gave only a topic scores 2 here.
   2: all kept; 1: one changed or missing; 0: more than one.
5. **Nothing of another kind crowds it out.** The lesson is not mostly a different kind of
   activity (a writing lesson that is mostly quiz questions).
   2: none; 1: one step of another kind without a heading of its own; 0: mostly another kind.

A step that carries a heading of its own (the teacher pressed several) is judged against its own
heading and kind.

## The total

Out of 10. **Fits**: 8 to 10. **Partly fits**: 5 to 7. **Does not fit**: 0 to 4. Write one
sentence on the lowest-scoring point.

## Agreement

Two researchers score the same fifth of the lessons without talking, and agreement is worked out
on the three bands (Cohen's kappa) and on each point. Disagreements are talked through; both
original scores are kept.
